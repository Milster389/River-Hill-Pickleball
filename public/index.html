Our Story

More Than a Club —
A Movement

River Hill Pickleball Club was founded in 2024 at River Hill High School in Clarksville, MD — making it the first high school pickleball club in all of Howard County.

What started as a vision to bring the fastest-growing sport in America to our school has grown into a community of 83+ members from all skill levels — from first-time players to competitive athletes.

The club was founded and continues to be led by Milan — a junior at River Hill HS, varsity athlete, and entrepreneur with a passion for building something that outlasts him.

  • Open to all skill levels — complete beginners welcome
  • Competitive team culture and tournament opportunities
  • Growing a sport that is one of the fastest-rising in the US
  • A welcoming space for every student at RHHS
  • Community outreach and local tournament representation
Our Mission
Grow the sport of pickleball in our school and community, build a competitive team culture, and create a welcoming space for everyone.
Where We Play
River Hill High School courts in Clarksville, MD — practices held every Tuesday and Thursday.
Who Can Join
Any River Hill HS student. No experience needed — just bring your energy. Equipment available for new players.
